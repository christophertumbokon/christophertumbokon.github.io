01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I'm a computer engineering student at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, with an expected graduation date of June 2028.

My interests center on embedded systems and firmware, particularly how software interacts with physical hardware. Through personal projects, I've worked with STM32 and ESP32 microcontrollers, communication interfaces, sensors, and real-time systems.

I've also worked as an undergraduate research assistant on a ROS 2 robotics project involving mobile robots, communication, and navigation. In addition, my IT support experience has helped me develop troubleshooting and collaboration skills.
